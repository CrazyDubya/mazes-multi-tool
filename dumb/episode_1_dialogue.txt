**Dialogue Document for Episode 1**

**Scene:** Alex Chen's apartment, evening. Alex is sitting at their desk, staring at their computer screen with a mixture of confusion and intrigue. The doorbell rings, and they get up to answer it.

**Alex:** (to the camera) I'm not sure what's going on, but I've been receiving some strange messages lately. This one was particularly cryptic. It read: "The truth is hidden in plain sight."

(Alex opens the door to reveal Detective James Reed standing on their porch.)

**Detective Reed:** Alex Chen? We need to talk.

**Alex:** (surprised) Detective Reed! What brings you here?

**Detective Reed:** I'm investigating a string of robberies and murders across the city. The latest victim was a woman who matched your... let's call them "social media profile."

**Alex:** (intrigued) Oh? Which social media profile?

**Detective Reed:** Sarah Taylor. Do you know her?

**Alex:** (hesitant) Yeah, I've followed her work for a while now. She's an influencer and advocate for various causes.

**Detective Reed:** That's right. And I think she might be connected to the case we're working on. Can I come in?

**Alex:** (sighing) Sure. But just for a minute. I have a lot of work to do tonight.

(Alex steps aside, allowing Detective Reed to enter. They sit down at Alex's desk, where Sarah Taylor is sitting with her laptop open.)

**Sarah:** Hi, James! Sorry I'm running late. Traffic was a nightmare.

**Detective Reed:** (ignoring Sarah) So, Alex, can you tell me more about your relationship with Sarah?

**Alex:** (diplomatically) We've met online a few times. She's... enthusiastic, to say the least.

**Sarah:** (laughing) Enthusiastic? That's one way to put it!

(The camera captures a brief exchange between Alex and Sarah, during which they seem to be discussing the details of the investigation.)

**Detective Reed:** Alright, I think we've covered enough for tonight. Can you give me a list of your contacts online, Alex?

**Alex:** (hesitant) I'm not sure that's necessary, Detective.

**Sarah:** (interrupting) Actually, James, I think that's a great idea. Alex is always talking about her social media following...

(The conversation continues, with the trio discussing the investigation and their individual connections to Sarah.)

**Detective Reed:** Alright, let's get back on track. We need to find out who this Mastermind person is and what they're after.

**Alex:** (thoughtfully) I think that might be more than just a simple heist, Detective.

**Sarah:** (excitedly) Oh, I know! It's like something from a movie!

(The camera captures the tension as the group begins to realize that this case may be more complex and sinister than they initially thought.)

Fade to black.
